# Second Audit — Phase 1 and First Substantive Tranche

> Adversarial audit of the official 9/11 narrative, with hypotheses H0–H7 kept separate, a documentary evidentiary standard, and explicit correction of the previous version ([`01-wtc-engineering-audit-v1.en.md`](01-wtc-engineering-audit-v1.en.md)).

> **Framing erratum (applied 19/09/2026).** Five formulations in this document were corrected by [`03-phase2a-mfr-verification.en.md`](03-phase2a-mfr-verification.en.md), section 1: the classification of H1, that of H5, the reading of Rice's statement about "all reporting pointed abroad", the inference about the other commissioners' access to the evidentiary corpus, and the separation between event date, document date and declassification date. The corrections are applied in the body below and marked **[corrected]**. General rule now in force: access restriction, generic warning and interested testimony are not, singly or together, proof of cover-up or of prior knowledge.

## 0. Method and the real ceiling of this session

Before any conclusion, the real access ceiling of this working session must be declared.

**It was not possible to read in full in this session:** the ~10,000 pages of NCSTAR 1 and annex volumes; the ~1,000 pages of NCSTAR 1A/1-9/1-9A; the complete Hulsey report; the 1,202 exhibits of *United States v. Zacarias Moussaoui*; the docket of *In re Terrorist Attacks on September 11, 2001*; the 585-page report of the Joint Congressional Inquiry; the SSCI Executive Summary on detention and interrogation; nor the complete set of Memoranda for the Record (MFRs) at NARA. That is an access and volume restriction of this specific session, not a statement about the existence or content of those documents.

What was done: access to and **full** reading of three primary documents, two of them declassified days before this session. Verification of the existence and content of three further repositories. Each recorded below with complete metadata, including access failures.

Prioritising the September 2026 release was deliberate: it is the only body of documents in the original scope that is genuinely new, of a size that permits honest full reading within one session, and that bears directly on H1, H4 and H5 — the hypotheses on which the first audit (`01`) was most silent, because it dealt only with engineering.

## 2. What was wrong, overstated or premature in the first audit (`01`)

**Conceded in full:**

1. **Use of the tower's total potential energy as if it resolved the progression.** It does not. A global energy budget is a necessary condition, not a sufficient one. The real question is stage by stage: effectively participating mass, mass ejected laterally out of the collapse front, compaction ratio, fall height available before first contact. The figure of 900 GJ does not demonstrate that stage 40 would occur.

2. **Floor-connection capacity presented as "independent verification".** It is not. That calculation answers whether **one** isolated floor could arrest the fall, treating the upper block as rigid and ignoring mass ejection, deformation of the block itself, and sequence. An order-of-magnitude argument about initiation, not about global progression.

3. **The lumped-capacitance calculation demonstrates class plausibility, not actual temperatures.** It shows that unprotected steel *can* reach 600 °C in minutes under post-flashover exposure. It does not establish the temperature of any specific element at any instant.

4. **Equating reduction of steel strength with elimination of the safety factor.** A conceptual error. What matters is the demand/capacity ratio of each element, the actual utilisation rate, load redistribution and thermal restraint.

5. **"No WTC 7 steel recovered" is wrong.** NIST states that samples were removed from the site before the investigation began and that WTC 7's steel did not carry the identifying markings that the towers' steel had. That is absence of positive identification, not absence of recovery.

6. **"Entirely model-based" was an overstatement.** There was video, photography, structural and erection drawings, fire observations and testimony. What was missing was identified steel for metallurgical correlation. The correct formulation: the specific causal chain (beam → Column 79 → cascade) is established by a model, calibrated against external observations.

7. **"Strong scientific consensus" without measurement.** No measurement was made — no opinion survey with a probability sample, no formal positions of ASCE/SEI, AISC, IStructE, no count of peer-reviewed papers contesting the mechanism, no citation analysis. **Statement withdrawn** and replaced by: no published challenge in a first-rank structural-engineering journal was observed in the literature consulted — which is an observation about the sample consulted, not a measure of consensus.

8. **Thermite, nanothermite, conventional explosives and mechanical cutting treated as a single hypothesis.** They are four hypotheses with completely different physical signatures, logistical requirements, acoustic footprints and residual evidence.

9. **The prior fall of the east penthouse presented as "the opposite" of every controlled demolition.** It is evidence of internal failure preceding façade failure. It excludes the classic bottom-up, façade-first demolition pattern. It does not exclude a sequence designed to initiate internally, nor a top-down demolition.

10. **Uncritical acceptance of NIST's 130–140 dB acoustic estimate.** A real acoustic audit would require: original recordings with known gain and distance, microphone frequency response, saturation/clipping analysis, urban background noise, and the minimum charge required for the specific failure mode in question.

11. **Sulphidic corrosion/eutectic in FEMA 403 Appendix C — mentioned without reading the document.** Appendix C was not read in this session. Structure of the evidentiary problem: intergranular attack with Fe-S eutectic formation requires available sulphur and sustained elevated temperature; known competing explanations include sulphur from drywall gypsum, from fuels, and prolonged exposure in the debris fire (which burned for weeks), against the hypothesis of an intentional sulphur-bearing agent (thermate). Discriminating requires knowing **when** the corrosion occurred — and that is what samples without a temporal chain of custody cannot establish. Deferred to a future technical phase.

**Conceded with qualification:**

12. Reclassification from "no evidence" for thermite to: **"contested evidence of low methodological quality, plus absence of targeted testing"** — NIST states explicitly that it did not test residues, and absence of testing is not a negative result.

**Not conceded:**

13. It stands that "the towers fell at free fall" is factually incorrect for the towers, and that confusing ejected perimeter panels with the crush front is an error of reading evidence, not an open question.

## 3. Substantive finding: what the September 2026 documents establish

### 3.1 The Commission restricted PDB access to its own leadership

DOC-1 establishes a highly relevant process fact: there were **353 PDB articles** between 1998 and 20/09/2001 touching on al-Qa'ida, UBL, Afghanistan, or terrorism involving Pakistan, Saudi Arabia or Sudan. A "core group" of **24 articles** dealing with possible attacks inside the United States and/or the use of aircraft was made available to a **four-person review team**, including the Commission's Chair and Vice-Chair. The remainder of the corpus was made available only to a **two-person subcommittee**.

**[corrected]** The earlier formulation — "no commissioner other than Kean and Hamilton, and no thematic team, had direct access to the corpus" — went beyond what the memorandum sustains and has been withdrawn. The memorandum establishes the structure of access **to the PDB corpus**; it does not establish what the other commissioners and the thematic teams received by other routes (NID/SEIB, MFRs, review-team briefings, derivative documents), nor whether there was a formal sharing mechanism. Those four determinations are pending and pre-registered in [`03`](03-phase2a-mfr-verification.en.md), section 2.5, together with the identification by name of the four Review Team members and the two subcommittee members — which the memorandum read does **not** name.

**Classification: proven** as to the structure of access to the PDB corpus (the document asserts this about itself). **Not determined** as to the other commissioners' access to derivative material, and therefore **not established** that independent verification was structurally impossible.

### 3.2 Aircraft as a weapon appeared in the PDB from 1998

DOC-1, in the core group:
- **10/09/1998**: a *walk-in* alleges that the group responsible for the Africa bombings may fly an explosives-laden aircraft into an American city.
- **04/12/1998**: "UBL Preparing to Hijack US Aircraft and Other Attacks". Mentions that two members of an operational team are said to have evaded security controls at an unidentified New York airport.
- **09/03/2000**: a seized manifesto from a cell recommending targets such as skyscrapers, airports or nuclear plants.

Counterweight from the same document: the PDB of 04/12/1998 notes that no member of the network had carried out a hijacking; that of 03/07/1998 describes the sources as "highly questionable, unreliable"; that of 06/08/2001 contained, **in bold in the original**, a caveat that CIA had been unable to corroborate the most sensational reporting, including the 1998 report about hijacking an aircraft to free the "Blind Shaykh".

DOC-3 corroborates and partly corrects: Scheuer, under oath ⁽ᵃ⁾, **did not recall** the September 1998 report about an explosives-laden aircraft, but states that aircraft-as-weapon was already a known issue for anyone working the UBL target, because of the intelligence on Murad and the plan to fly a plane into CIA headquarters. He recalled the November 1998 report about a hijacking on the east coast, widely disseminated — and adds that he took part in no conversation assessing the reliability of that source, nor in the analysis of the report.

The fine analytical finding: the documented problem is not absence of information, it is **absence of a process for assessing** the information that existed.

### 3.3 Rice: one retraction and several documented tensions

**Explicit retraction.** Rice tells the Commission that she misspoke at the May 2002 press conference in saying that no one could have imagined the use of planes as missiles; she should have said that *she* could not have imagined it, and learned afterwards of earlier reporting on that tactic. **Classification: proven** (a recorded retraction of a widely quoted public statement).

In the same passage, the Commission's General Counsel records that Italian authorities closed the airspace over Genoa during the G-8 summit in the summer of 2001, out of concern about planes used as missiles. Rice replies that she does not know why no one made the imaginative leap.

**Tension with DOC-1.** Rice states that there was no stream of threat reporting about attacks inside the United States, and that all the reporting pointed abroad. DOC-1 documents 24 PDB articles whose declared subject is precisely possible attacks inside the United States and/or the use of aircraft. Possible reconciliations: Rice may be speaking of the summer 2001 stream specifically; the source quality of several of those 24 articles was explicitly poor. **[corrected] Classification: unresolved documentary tension.** The earlier classification ("contradicted in its absolute form") has been withdrawn: it presupposes a comparison this audit has not yet made. Closing the question requires four items, none of them available in the sources read: (i) the full text of the PDB of 06/08/2001, not the Commission's description of it; (ii) the precise chronology of the reporting stream in the summer of 2001, month by month; (iii) the reliability the sources themselves attributed to each report; (iv) the actual distribution of the 353 articles between domestic and foreign threat — the memorandum gives the thematic cut of 24 articles, not the distribution of the corpus.

**Characterisation of the PDB of 06/08/2001.** Rice describes the article as containing no current information at all, and characterises the contact with the UAE embassy as apparently a hoax. DOC-1 shows that the PDB stated that the FBI was conducting about **70 full field investigations** related to UBL in the United States, and that CIA and FBI were **investigating** the call to the UAE — it does not describe it as a hoax. **Classification: the witness's characterisation in tension with the text of the document.** Caveat: the original PDB was not read — only the Commission's description of it in DOC-1.

**Three checking notes from the Commission's own staff**, inserted in the MFR: Rice said she had never seen Predator video, whereas Barton Gellman reported that video was shown to her on 10/01/2001; the Commission has no evidence that the draft MON/finding was shown to other principals; Rice's account of the timing of her call to the President on 11/09 differs from what she told Evan Thomas on 11/11/2001, with the Situation Room log and the Presidential Diary recording the call at 09:40. This documents imprecision by the witness **and** documents that the Commission's staff were actively checking the testimony against other sources.

**Bandar.** Rice was asked about a call from Prince Bandar at 12:25 on 11/09. She describes it as non-substantive, condolences. She says she does not recall the evacuation of Saudi citizens coming up in the call, that she knew Saudis left the country but does not recall who authorised it, and that she learned of the matter from the press. A record of non-recollection, neither refutation nor confirmation.

**Saudi money.** Questioned by Warren Bass, Rice said she had no clarity as to whether **government** Saudi money went to NGOs supporting terrorism, but left the possibility open; she said she was certain that money from **private** Saudi citizens did. Relevant to H2/H3.

### 3.4 Scheuer under oath ⁽ᵃ⁾: the failure is of analytical process, not of information

**Absence of strategic analysis.** Scheuer states under oath ⁽ᵃ⁾ that throughout his time at CTC, until mid-1999, there was no strategic or synthetic analytical product on UBL's and al-Qa'ida's attack methods, including WMD; he repeatedly asked for an NIE and did not get one; he cites his own memorandum of 28/06/1999 documenting this. His phrase: they had more intelligence on UBL in the summer of 1998 than on any other subject in the Agency's history, and no one was interested.

If true, strong and specific evidence for **H4** (failure through fragmentation and priorities) and evidence **against H5** (specific operational knowledge deliberately unused): the pathology described is absence of synthesis, not suppression of a product that existed.

**One documented instance of institutional self-protection shaping the PDB.** Scheuer reports that a text on WMD was barred from the PDB of 25/03/1999 at the DCI's direction, because it exposed an embarrassing two-year delay between the FBI obtaining the information and passing it to CIA. He names the PDB's author and the corresponding memorandum. **This is H1 in pure form, and prior to the attack** — not subsequent, as the usual formulation of "cover-up" tends to presuppose. **Classification: a claim under oath ⁽ᵃ⁾, with a verifiable documentary reference, not verified in this session.**

**The 30% vs 0% discrepancy.** On the UBL capture operation cancelled in May 1998: Scheuer states that he and another officer briefed Clarke, Berger, Reno and Freeh using an estimate of a 30% chance of success, and that he received a call from Patrick Fitzgerald and others reporting that Mary Jo White had been told **0%**. Since, on Scheuer's reading, Reno depended on White's assessment, the discrepancy would have been decisive. **Classification: a claim under oath ⁽ᵃ⁾ by an interested witness, with named and verifiable witnesses.**

**On the Saudis.** Scheuer describes them under oath ⁽ᵃ⁾ as extremely uncooperative and obstructive; states that he never received a single useful piece of information; cites the case of Madani al-Tayyib, a UBL associate to whom the Saudis gave shelter; cites two of his own documents (memo of 03/05/1996 and Spot Report of 24/06/1997). He adds his belief that one of the reasons for the cancellation of the May 1998 operation was the Saudi offer to resolve the UBL problem themselves. Favours **H2/H3** on the dimension of **non-cooperation and diplomatic protection**, categorically distinct from conscious logistical support for the attack.

**Important calibration.** The same witness rejects, in the same testimony, a popular alternative hypothesis: he calls "too conspiratorial" the idea of an instrumental relationship between the Pakistani service and UBL for training, attributing radicalisation to long-run processes that preceded it. This increases Scheuer's credibility as a witness.

**Counter-argument to record.** Scheuer's account has the structure of a personal vindication narrative — he was the target of an attempt to dismantle his unit, excluded from leadership meetings, prevented from travelling, cut out of an after-action review. Testimony in which the witness was right about everything and all his superiors were wrong deserves a discount, even under oath. What raises its weight are the specific, dated documentary references — memos with dates and addressees — falsifiable by anyone with access to the archive.

## 6. Audit of the Commission's process — first verified findings

- **PDB access:** restricted to 4 people (core group) and 2 people (full corpus). Verified in DOC-1.
- **Testimony under oath:** heterogeneous, verifiable document by document. Scheuer: under oath ⁽ᵃ⁾, recorded. Rice (February 2004, Situation Room): the MFR **records no oath**. Rice's public hearing, under oath, took place later, in April 2004 (not verified in this session). A career official under oath ⁽ᵃ⁾, the National Security Adviser in a meeting with no oath recorded at the White House — a relevant asymmetry of process.
- **Location of the testimony:** Rice's took place in the Situation Room, with four members of the Executive Office of the President's staff present. Testimony at the witness's own facility with her staff present affects candour.
- **Record:** MFRs are syntheses written by staff members, with pending-revision marks. They are not transcripts. Any use of them as direct quotation is improper.
- **Active checking:** the staff inserted notes confronting the testimony with presidential logs, journalism, and absence of corroborating evidence.
- **Bush/Cheney:** interview released only in a redacted version, in 2022, and **not included** in the 2026 release.
- **Independence:** not audited in this session. Warren Bass, author of Rice's MFR, and Philip Zelikow, Executive Director, took part in the meeting. Zelikow co-authored a book with Rice (*Germany Unified and Europe Transformed*, 1995) — a fact recorded in the MFR itself, in the witness's biography. An apparent conflict of interest that the document exposes inadvertently; deserves treatment in a future phase.
