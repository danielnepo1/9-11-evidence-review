## 2. Procedural status — not an interview, and not under oath

Printed p.1, image-verified:

The form field is the Commission's own, and it distinguishes this event from the "Interview" typed in DOC-7 and DOC-8. No oath is recorded, requested or referred to anywhere in the three pages.

Registered in [`../witness-status.md`](../witness-status.md). Permitted label: **meeting memorandum**. Never "testimony"; never "interview record".

### 2.1 This resolves DISC-008

DOC-8's front matter states that the witness "was placed under oath at the start of **his first interview on January 22, 2004**". [DISC-008](../discrepancies.md#disc-008) recorded that the ISCAP index lists a Tenet MFR of 23 December 2003, and asked whether the front matter was therefore inaccurate — a contingency that **eighteen U-rows inherited**, because DOC-8's `oath: carried over` rests on it.

**It is accurate.** The 23 December 2003 event was a luncheon meeting, not an interview, on the Commission's own typing. Page 3 confirms the interviews were still prospective at that date: *"On procedures, there would be an interview on classified topics, led by staff with participation by commissioners… There would then be a public hearing in March."*

**The contingency on the eighteen U-rows is discharged.** DOC-8's carried-over oath is sound.

### 6.1 What this does to DISC-007

[DISC-007](../discrepancies.md#disc-007) recorded that DOC-7's footnote 1 attributes to "the DCI's follow-up session with the Commission, on January 28th, 2004" a discussion of the President's edits to the February 1999 MON — content absent from DOC-8, which **is** the MFR of 28 January 2004.

Two things settle it:

1. **DOC-7 printed p.12, now image-verified.** The footnote reads **"January 28th, 2004"** with no ambiguity. The digit is clear on the page image; the earlier text-layer uncertainty is gone.
2. **DOC-9 contains nothing on the subject** — zero hits for `February 1999`, `1999 MON` or `Masood`.

**EC1 is excluded.** The footnote does not name a different session, and no other session in the corpus carries the content. The remaining live explanation is **EC2 — ordinary selective note-taking** by a single staff drafter across a multi-hour session.

> **The finding therefore stands, and it is the calibration result the reading program was run to obtain: an MFR is not a complete record of its session.** One instance is confirmed. The silence of an MFR is evidence about the MFR, not about what the session covered.
>
> **What this does not establish.** Nothing about concealment. The omitted passage is *favourable* to the witness — it explains why CIA did not press the White House on the MON — which cuts against any adverse reading. And one confirmed instance does not establish a general practice; that needs further MFR pairs where a footnote can be checked against its own session's record.
