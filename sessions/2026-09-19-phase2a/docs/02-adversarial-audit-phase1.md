# Second Audit — Phase 1 and First Substantive Tranche

> Adversarial audit of the official 9/11 narrative, with hypotheses H0–H7 kept separate, a documentary evidentiary standard, and explicit correction of the previous version ([`01-wtc-engineering-audit-v1.md`](01-wtc-engineering-audit-v1.md)).

> **Framing errata (applied 19/09/2026).** Five formulations in this document were corrected by [`03-phase2a-mfr-verification.md`](03-phase2a-mfr-verification.md), section 1: the classification of H1, that of H5, the reading of Rice's statement on "all reporting pointed abroad", the inference about the other commissioners' access to the evidentiary corpus, and the separation between event date, document date, and declassification date. The corrections are applied in the body below and marked **[corrected]**. General rule now in force: access restriction, generic warning, and interested testimony are not, alone or combined, proof of cover-up or of prior knowledge. See also the impartiality rule in [`README.md`](../README.md): the official account is not treated as a true baseline, and evidentiary gaps are never themselves treated as proof of an alternative hypothesis.

## 0. Method and the real ceiling of this session

Before any conclusion, it is necessary to state the real access ceiling of this working session.

**Could not be read in full in this session:** the ~10,000 pages of NCSTAR 1 and its annex volumes; the ~1,000 pages of NCSTAR 1A/1-9/1-9A; the complete Hulsey report; the 1,202 exhibits of *United States v. Zacarias Moussaoui*; the docket of *In re Terrorist Attacks on September 11, 2001*; the 585-page Joint Congressional Inquiry report; the SSCI Executive Summary on detention and interrogation; nor the complete set of Memoranda for the Record (MFRs) at NARA. This is an access and volume constraint of this specific session, not a claim about the existence or content of those documents.

What was done: access to and **full** reading of three primary documents, two of them declassified days before this session. Verification of the existence and content of three further repositories. Each is logged below with full metadata, including access failures.

Prioritizing the September 2026 release was deliberate: it is the only documentary body within the original scope that is genuinely new, of a size that allows an honestly complete reading within one session, and that bears directly on H1, H4, and H5 — the hypotheses where the first audit (`01`) was most deficient, having dealt only with engineering.

## 2. What was wrong, overstated, or premature in the first audit (`01`)

**Fully conceded:**

1. **Using the tower's total potential energy as if it settled the progression.** It does not. A global energy budget is a necessary, not sufficient, condition. The real question is stage by stage: mass actually participating, mass ejected laterally that exits the collapse front, compaction ratio, fall height available before first contact. The figure of 900 GJ does not demonstrate that stage 40 would occur.

2. **Floor connection capacity presented as an "independent check".** It is not. That calculation answers whether **one** isolated floor could stop the fall, treating the upper block as rigid and ignoring mass ejection, deformation of the block itself, and sequencing. An order-of-magnitude argument about initiation, not about global progression.

3. **The lumped-capacitance calculation demonstrates class plausibility, not real temperatures.** It shows that unprotected steel *can* reach 600 °C within minutes under post-flashover exposure. It does not establish the temperature of any specific element at any specific instant.

4. **Equating a reduction in steel strength with elimination of the safety factor.** A conceptual error. What matters is each element's demand/capacity ratio, its actual utilization rate, load redistribution, and thermal restraint.

5. **"No WTC 7 steel recovered" is wrong.** NIST states that samples were removed from the site before the investigation began and that WTC 7's steel lacked the identifying markings that the towers' steel had. This is an absence of positive identification, not an absence of recovery.

6. **"Entirely model-based" was an overstatement.** There was video, photography, structural and erection drawings, fire observations, and testimony. What was missing was identified steel for metallurgical correlation. The correct formulation: the specific causal chain (beam → Column 79 → cascade) is established by a model, calibrated against external observations.

7. **"Strong scientific consensus" without measurement.** No measurement was made — a probability-sample opinion survey, formal positions from ASCE/SEI, AISC, IStructE, a count of peer-reviewed articles disputing the mechanism, citation analysis. **Claim withdrawn** and replaced with: no challenge published in a top-tier structural-engineering journal was observed in the literature consulted — which is an observation about the sample consulted, not a measure of consensus.

8. **Thermite, nanothermite, conventional explosives, and mechanical cutting treated as a single hypothesis.** These are four hypotheses with completely different physical signatures, logistical requirements, acoustic footprints, and residual evidence.

9. **The earlier fall of the east penthouse presented as "the opposite" of all controlled demolition.** It is evidence of internal failure prior to the façade failure. It rules out the classic bottom-up, façade-first demolition pattern. It does not rule out a sequence designed to initiate internally, nor top-down demolition.

10. **Uncritical acceptance of NIST's 130–140 dB acoustic estimate.** A real acoustic audit would require: original recordings with known gain and distance, microphone frequency response, saturation/clipping analysis, background urban noise, and the minimum charge needed for the specific failure mode in question.

11. **Sulfidic corrosion/eutectic in FEMA 403 Appendix C — mentioned without reading the document.** Appendix C was not read in this session. Structure of the evidentiary problem: intergranular attack with Fe-S eutectic formation requires available sulfur and sustained elevated temperature; known competing explanations include sulfur from drywall gypsum, from fuels, and from prolonged exposure in the debris fire (which burned for weeks), against the hypothesis of an intentional sulfur-bearing agent (thermate). Discriminating requires knowing **when** the corrosion occurred — which is what samples lacking a temporal chain of custody cannot establish. Left pending for a future technical phase.

**Conceded with qualification:**

12. Reclassification from "no evidence" for thermite to: **"contested evidence of low methodological quality, plus absence of targeted testing"** — NIST explicitly states that it did not test for residues, and absence of testing is not a negative result.

**Not conceded:**

13. It remains the case that "the towers fell in free fall" is factually incorrect for the towers, and that confusing ejected perimeter panels with the crush front is a misreading of the evidence, not an open question.

## 3. Substantive finding: what the September 2026 documents establish

### 3.1 The Commission restricted PDB access to its own top tier

DOC-1 establishes a highly relevant process fact: there were **353 PDB articles** between 1998 and 20/09/2001 touching on al-Qa'ida, UBL, Afghanistan, or terrorism involving Pakistan, Saudi Arabia, or Sudan. A "core group" of **24 articles** dealing with possible attacks inside the US and/or the use of aircraft was made available to a **four-person review team**, including the Commission's Chair and Vice-Chair. The rest of the corpus was made available only to a **two-person subcommittee**.

**[corrected]** The earlier formulation — "no commissioner other than Kean and Hamilton, and no thematic team, had direct access to the corpus" — went beyond what the memorandum supports and has been withdrawn. The memorandum establishes the access structure **for the PDB corpus**; it does not establish what the other commissioners and thematic teams received through other channels (NID/SEIB, MFRs, review-team briefings, derivative documents), nor whether there was a formal sharing mechanism. These four determinations are pending and pre-registered in [`03`](03-phase2a-mfr-verification.md), section 2.5, along with the named identification of the four Review Team members and the two subcommittee members — whom the memorandum read **does not** name.

**Classification: proven** as to the access structure for the PDB corpus (the document itself states this about itself). **Not determined** as to the other commissioners' access to derivative material, and therefore **not established** that independent verification was structurally impossible.

### 3.2 Aircraft-as-weapon appeared in the PDB as early as 1998

DOC-1, in the core group:
- **10/09/1998**: a *walk-in* alleges that the group responsible for the Africa attacks may fly an explosives-laden plane into an American city.
- **04/12/1998**: "UBL Preparing to Hijack US Aircraft and Other Attacks". Mentions that two members of an operational team allegedly evaded security controls at an unidentified New York airport.
- **09/03/2000**: a seized cell manifesto recommending targets such as skyscrapers, airports, or nuclear plants.

Counterweight from the same document: the 04/12/1998 PDB notes that no member of the network had carried out a hijacking; the 03/07/1998 PDB describes the sources as "highly questionable, unreliable"; the 06/08/2001 PDB contained, **in bold in the original**, a caveat that the CIA had been unable to corroborate the more sensational reports, including the 1998 one about hijacking an aircraft to free the "Blind Shaykh".

DOC-3 partially corroborates and corrects: Scheuer, under oath, **did not recall** the September 1998 report about an explosives-laden aircraft, but states that aircraft-as-weapon was already a known issue to anyone working the UBL target, because of intelligence on Murad and the plan to fly a plane into CIA headquarters. He did recall the November 1998 report about a hijacking on the East Coast, which was widely disseminated — and adds that he took part in no conversation to assess the reliability of that source or the analysis of the report.

The fine-grained analytical finding: the documented problem is not an absence of information, it is an **absence of a process for assessing** existing information.

### 3.3 Rice: one retraction and several documented tensions

**Explicit retraction.** Rice tells the Commission that she misspoke at the May 2002 press conference when she said that no one could have imagined the use of planes as missiles; she should have said that *she* could not have imagined it, and that she learned afterward of earlier reports about that tactic. **Classification: proven** (retraction on the record of a widely cited public statement).

In the same passage, the Commission's General Counsel notes that Italian authorities closed the airspace over Genoa during the G-8 summit in the summer of 2001, out of concern about planes used as missiles. Rice responds that she does not know why no one made that imaginative leap.

**Tension with DOC-1.** Rice states that there was no flow of threat reporting about attacks inside the US, and that all reports pointed abroad. DOC-1 documents 24 PDB articles whose stated theme is exactly possible attacks inside the US and/or the use of aircraft. Possible reconciliations: Rice may be speaking specifically about the summer 2001 flow; the source quality of several of those 24 articles was explicitly poor. **[corrected] Classification: unresolved documentary tension.** The earlier classification ("contradicted in absolute form") has been withdrawn: it presupposes a comparison this audit has not yet made. Closing the question requires four items, none available in the sources read: (i) the full text of the 06/08/2001 PDB, not the Commission's description of it; (ii) the precise chronology of the reporting flow in the summer of 2001, month by month; (iii) the reliability the sources themselves attributed to each report; (iv) the real distribution of the 353 articles between domestic and external threat — the memorandum gives the 24-article thematic slice, not the distribution of the corpus.

**Characterization of the 06/08/2001 PDB.** Rice describes the article as containing no current information whatsoever, and characterizes the contact with the UAE embassy as apparently a hoax. DOC-1 shows that the PDB stated that the FBI was conducting about **70 full field investigations** related to UBL inside the US, and that CIA and FBI were **investigating** the call to the UAE — it does not qualify it as a hoax. **Classification: witness characterization in tension with the document's text.** Caveat: the original PDB was not read — only the Commission's description in DOC-1.

**Three fact-checking notes from the Commission's own staff**, inserted in the MFR: Rice said she never saw Predator video, while Barton Gellman reported that she was shown video on 10/01/2001; the Commission has no evidence that MON/finding drafts were shown to other *principals*; Rice's account of the timing of her call with the President on 9/11 differs from what she told Evan Thomas on 11/11/2001, with the Situation Room log and the President's Daily Diary recording the call at 9:40 a.m. This documents inaccuracies by the witness **and** documents that the Commission's staff was actively checking the testimony against other sources.

**Bandar.** Rice was asked about a call from Prince Bandar at 12:25 p.m. on 9/11. She describes it as non-substantive, a condolence call. She says she does not recall the evacuation of Saudi citizens coming up in the call, that she knew Saudis left the country but does not recall who authorized it, and that she learned of the matter from the press. A record of non-recollection, neither refutation nor confirmation.

**Saudi money.** Asked by Warren Bass, Rice said she was not clear on whether money from the Saudi **government** went to NGOs supporting terrorism, but left the possibility open; she said she was certain that money from Saudi **private citizens** did. Relevant to H2/H3.

### 3.4 Scheuer under oath: the failure is analytic-process, not information

**Absence of strategic analysis.** Scheuer states under oath that throughout his time at CTC, until mid-1999, there was no strategic or synthetic analytic product on UBL's and al-Qa'ida's attack methods, including WMD; he repeatedly requested an NIE and did not get one; he cites his own memorandum of 28/06/1999 documenting this. His words: they had more intelligence on UBL in the summer of 1998 than at any other point in the Agency's history, and no one was interested.

If true, this is strong, specific evidence for **H4** (failure through fragmentation and misplaced priorities) and evidence **against H5** (specific operational knowledge deliberately not used): the pathology described is an absence of synthesis, not suppression of a product that existed.

**One documented instance of institutional self-protection shaping the PDB.** Scheuer states that a WMD-related passage was removed from the 25/03/1999 PDB on the DCI's direction, because it exposed an embarrassing two-year delay between the FBI obtaining the information and passing it to the CIA. He cites the PDB's author and the corresponding memorandum. **This is H1 in pure form, and it precedes the attack** — not after the fact, as the usual "cover-up" framing tends to assume. **Classification: sworn allegation, with a verifiable documentary reference, not verified in this session.**

**The 30% vs. 0% discrepancy.** On the UBL capture operation cancelled in May 1998: Scheuer states that he and another officer briefed Clarke, Berger, Reno, and Freeh using an estimate of a 30% chance of success, and that he received a call from Patrick Fitzgerald and others reporting that Mary Jo White had been told **0%**. Since, on Scheuer's reading, Reno relied on White's assessment, the discrepancy would have been decisive. **Classification: sworn allegation from an interested witness, with named and verifiable witnesses.**

**On the Saudis.** Scheuer describes them under oath as extremely uncooperative and obstructionist; he states he never received a single piece of useful information; he cites the case of Madani al-Tayyib, a UBL associate to whom the Saudis gave sanctuary; he cites two of his own documents (a memo of 03/05/1996 and a Spot Report of 24/06/1997). He adds his belief that one of the reasons for the cancellation of the May 1998 operation was a Saudi offer to resolve the UBL problem on their own. This favors **H2/H3** on the dimension of **non-cooperation and diplomatic protection**, categorically distinct from conscious logistical support for the attack.

**An important calibration.** The same witness rejects, in the same testimony, a popular alternative hypothesis: he calls "too conspiratorial" the idea of an instrumental relationship between the Pakistani service and UBL for training, attributing radicalization to long-term prior processes. This increases Scheuer's credibility as a witness.

**A counter-argument to record.** Scheuer's account has the structure of a personal-vindication narrative — he was the target of an attempt to dismantle his unit, excluded from leadership meetings, blocked from travel, cut out of a post-action review. Testimony in which the witness was right about everything and all his superiors were wrong deserves a discount, even under oath. What raises its weight are the specific, dated documentary references — memos with dates and addressees — falsifiable by anyone with access to the file.

## 6. Audit of the Commission's process — first verified findings

- **PDB access:** restricted to 4 people (core group) and 2 people (full corpus). Verified in DOC-1.
- **Sworn testimony:** heterogeneous, verifiable document by document. Scheuer: under oath, on the record. Rice (February 2004, Situation Room): the MFR **does not record an oath**. Rice's sworn public hearing took place later, in April 2004 (not verified in this session). A sworn career official, a National Security Advisor in an unsworn White House meeting — a relevant process asymmetry.
- **Location of the testimony:** Rice's took place in the Situation Room, with four members of the Executive Office of the President's staff present. Testimony on the witness's own premises, with her staff present, affects candor.
- **Record:** MFRs are syntheses drafted by staff members, with pending-review marks. They are not transcripts. Any use of them as a direct quotation is improper.
- **Active fact-checking:** the staff inserted notes cross-checking the testimony against presidential logs, press reporting, and the absence of corroborating evidence.
- **Bush/Cheney:** interview released only in redacted form, in 2022, and **not included** in the 2026 release.
- **Independence:** not audited in this session. Warren Bass, author of the Rice MFR, and Philip Zelikow, Executive Director, took part in the meeting. Zelikow co-authored a book with Rice (*Germany Unified and Europe Transformed*, 1995) — a fact recorded in the MFR itself, in the witness's biography. An apparent conflict of interest that the document exposes inadvertently; it deserves treatment in a future phase.
