# Evidence Matrix

> **No claim may advance to a conclusion until it appears here with every cell filled.** This file exists to stop a reading from becoming a verdict. A document read is an input; a claim resolved requires supporting evidence, contrary evidence, recorded absence of evidence, and stated uncertainty — all four, each traced to a primary source read under a document card.

Created 19 September 2026. Governed by [`04`](04-methodology-symmetric-skepticism.md). Cards in [`cards/`](cards/).

---

## 1. Required cells

A row is **not** resolvable until all of these are filled. `not determined` is a valid entry; blank is not.

| Cell | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Claim** | The atomic proposition, as worded in `03` §5.1 or in the claim register |
| **Claimant / date / oath** | Who asserted it, when, under what status |
| **Primary sources read that bear on it** | Card IDs only. A source not read under a card may not appear |
| **Supporting evidence** | Exact passage and page, from a card |
| **Contrary evidence** | Exact passage and page, from a card |
| **Absence of evidence** | What was looked for in the sources read and **was not there**, stated as absence and never as denial |
| **Uncertainty** | Hedges in the sources, source-reliability limits, redactions, interpretive divergence |
| **Discriminating item still missing** | The single item that would most move the row |
| **Status** | Closed vocabulary (`04` §4.2). Assigned only when every cell above is filled |

|---|---|

| Supporting evidence | **Partial, for one half only.** The PDB's substance is predominantly historical: its datable items run 1993–1999 except two (`PDB-20010806` §5) |
| Contrary evidence | On the hoax half: the PDB says "CIA and the FBI **are investigating** a call to our Embassy in the UAE in May" (p.2). It records an open investigation and assesses nothing. The date and author of any hoax assessment are unknown. Three source-identifying redactions limit reliability weighting |
| Discriminating item still missing | **The dated record of the hoax assessment** — who reached it, on what basis, and before or after 6 August 2001. Second: the verbatim primary transcript of Rice's characterisation, which this audit has still not read in the original |
