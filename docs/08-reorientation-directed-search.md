# 08 — Reorientation: Directed Search Against the Official Account

> **This document changes the project's objective. It does not change any finding, and it does not lower any evidentiary standard.** Read alongside [`04-methodology-symmetric-skepticism.md`](04-methodology-symmetric-skepticism.md), which it supersedes on objective and resource priority only.

Session date: **20 September 2026**.

---

## 1. What happened

The user asked directly: "How does this get us to the final objective — finding proof or irrefutable signs that put the official account in question? That is my objective." Asked to choose explicitly between keeping the symmetric framework, reorienting to a directed search, or a middle path, the user chose: **reorient to a directed search that actively prioritizes evidence weakening the official account**, with the tradeoff stated and accepted in advance — resources are no longer split evenly across H0–H7, and the output is understood to be a **thesis/advocacy case**, not a neutral audit.

This is recorded as the user's informed decision, made after the tradeoff was put to them plainly. It is executed as given.

---

## 2. What changes

| Item | Before (`04`) | Now |
|---|---|---|
| **Objective** | Test the official account and every alternative with the same burden of proof; judge without a fixed prior conclusion | Find and present the strongest, best-evidenced case that specific claims in the official account are false, unsupported, or resting on a thinner base than their confident presentation implies |
| **Resource allocation** | Split across H0–H7; H0 decomposed and tested as hard as any alternative | Prioritized toward the workstreams and targets with the highest potential to produce a fracture in the official account |
| **Phase reporting** | Both "findings that weaken the official account" and "findings that weaken alternative accounts" mandatory, every phase | The first section stays mandatory. **The second becomes optional** — kept where it falls out of the same reading at no extra cost, dropped where pursuing it would only serve symmetry rather than the stated objective |
| **H1–H7 mirror testing (`06` Phase E / `NEXT-STEPS` step 3)** | Required, run against the same evidence as H0 | De-prioritized, not deleted. Revisited only if it bears on strengthening a specific case against the official account |
| **Register symmetry checks** (`anomaly-register.md` §6 "balance check") | A one-sided register was treated as a sign of one-sided reading, to be corrected | No longer a correction trigger. A one-sided register is now the expected shape of the work and is not, by itself, a defect |

## 3. What is explicitly preserved, and why

Dropping **hypothesis-neutrality** (equal effort across H0–H7) is not the same thing as dropping **evidentiary rigor** (how a single piece of evidence is verified before it is used). The second is what makes a finding resistant to the standard rebuttal — "that's just bureaucratic sloppiness," "that's just a scope mismatch," "that's not what the document says." A finding that survives that rebuttal is what "irrefutable" actually requires. Weakening rigor would make the output *more* refutable, which works against the stated objective, not for it. Nothing below is negotiable under this reorientation:

| Preserved instrument | File | Why it stays |
|---|---|---|
| Document-card standard, ten required sections | [`cards/README.md`](cards/README.md) | The record of what a source actually says, including what it does *not* say — the section most often skipped by advocacy writing that overclaims |
| Image verification of every quotation | [`decisions.md`](decisions.md) D-003 | An OCR error or an agency-OCR artefact quoted without checking is a free win for a rebuttal |
| Closed status vocabulary (`corroborated` / `contradicted` / `inconclusive` / etc.) | [`claim-decomposition-register.md`](claim-decomposition-register.md) §2 | Prevents laundering "suggestive" into "proven" |
| Evidence-matrix requirement: a row needs supporting evidence, contrary evidence, absence-of-evidence, and stated uncertainty before any status is assigned | [`evidence-matrix.md`](evidence-matrix.md) §1 | A case built without looking for its own counter-evidence is a case an opponent finds it for you, in public |
| The mirror test (M1–M5) | [`04`](04-methodology-symmetric-skepticism.md) §5.3, [`05`](05-stress-test-execution-plan.md) §3 | **Repurposed, not dropped.** Its job now is to pre-empt the ordinary-explanation rebuttal *before* a finding is published, not to enforce symmetry between hypotheses |
| Conclusion ceilings | [`05`](05-stress-test-execution-plan.md) §7 | Never claim more than a source supports. An overclaimed finding is the easiest kind to discredit, and discrediting one finding is routinely used to discredit all of them |
| Three-dates rule, source-genealogy checks, quantifier-drift audit | [`04`](04-methodology-symmetric-skepticism.md) §2.2; [`05`](05-stress-test-execution-plan.md) §3 | Unchanged — these test whether evidence is what it appears to be, independent of which direction it points |
| B1/B2 validation discipline | [`decisions.md`](decisions.md) D-001, D-002 | B1 stays unvalidated and unpublished regardless of objective. A wrong number is a wrong number whichever side it would have favored |

## 4. The one constraint this reorientation adds

**Framing disclosure is mandatory in any future synthesis.** A report that argues one side while presenting itself as a neutral comparison is dishonest to its reader, and that is a line this project does not cross regardless of objective. Every future synthesis document must state plainly, near the top, that it is a directed case built to challenge specific claims in the official account — not a symmetric audit — so a reader can weigh it accordingly. This is the one place the reorientation constrains rather than frees.
