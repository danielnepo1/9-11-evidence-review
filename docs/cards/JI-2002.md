### 1.1 Source character

Earliest of the three institutional reconstructions. Its account of the January 2000 chain rests on **testimony** (a CTC Supervisor, the DCI, the CTC Chief) and on **the Joint Inquiry's own record search at FBI Headquarters**, plus one contemporaneous e-mail quoted at length. It predates the DOJ OIG review and cites no other body — **genealogically independent** of `DOJOIG-2004` and `CR-2004`.

Actors are named by role only ("CTC Supervisor", "CIA employee", "the first/second FBI agent"), not by pseudonym.

## 10. Evidentiary ceiling

**What the pages read can support:** that in 2002, under oath, a CIA supervisor characterised the "passed to the FBI" cable as unconfirmable as to passage or receipt; that the Joint Inquiry independently found no record of the visa at FBI HQ; that the contemporaneous e-mails on both sides omit the visa; and that the CIA's institutional voice in 2002 was "not withholding it."

**What they cannot support:** anything about the CIR or the hold, which the JI does not mention; any date more precise than "early 2000"; any inference from the 2002–2004 shift in the CIA's position beyond the fact of the shift — which has an ordinary explanation (different speakers; an institutional response to an IG draft) that is untested; anything about the 824 pages not read.
