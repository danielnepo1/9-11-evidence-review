### 1.1 Source character — stated plainly

This document is **secondary** for T-2.1's purpose. It is an investigative body describing records that this audit has not read. Where it quotes a record verbatim, that quotation is the strongest available proxy for the record and is marked as such. Nothing below may be cited as "the cable says"; it is cited as "the DOJ OIG, with access to the cables, reports that the cable says."

Pseudonyms are the OIG's own: "Dwight" (FBI special agent detailed to the CIA Bin Laden Unit), "Michelle" (CIA desk officer, Bin Laden Unit), "John" (CIA Deputy Chief, Bin Laden Unit), "James" (CIA employee detailed to FBI HQ), "Bob" (FBI SSA, UBL Unit, FBI HQ), "Eric", "Malcolm". The OIG states at fn. 172 that "The CIA has asked the OIG not to identify the true names of CIA employees for operational reasons."

| Group | What the OIG declares its basis to be |

| G1–G4, G8, G9, G11 | **CIA records and the cable system itself** — "CIA records show…", timestamps of drafting, access and dissemination. The OIG had access to the system |
| G5–G7 | **The text of the fifth cable**, quoted in part |

| G14, G17 | The OIG's own search, and the CIA OIG's, for corroborating witnesses and documents |

**The load-bearing propositions rest on contemporaneous records, not on recollection.** G5, G8, G9, G11, G18 and G19 are all record-based. The recollection evidence (G15, G16, G21–G23) is uniformly negative — everyone says they remember nothing — and therefore adds nothing in either direction.

## 4. What the document does not say

| Not present in what was read | Note |
|---|---|
| That anyone deliberately withheld the information | The OIG attributes "primary responsibility for the failure" to the CIA and says "the evidence indicates that the CIA did not provide permission for the CIR to be sent" (PDF 366). It does not characterise that as intentional concealment |
| That the fifth cable was knowingly false | The OIG says it found no evidence the cable "was correct". That is a statement about corroboration, not about the author's knowledge |
| Who told Michelle the documents had been passed | Not determined. G15 |
| Why the Deputy Chief directed the hold | Not determined. G22 — he did not recall it |
| Whether the hold and the fifth cable are causally connected | **The OIG states the time relationship (G8) and draws no causal inference from it.** Neither does this card |
| The exact calendar date of the drafting and the hold | The passages read say "the same morning"/"the same day" relative to Mihdhar's 5 January 2000 arrival and the cable traffic of 5–6 January. **The precise date was not established from what was read** and should be pinned from the unread pages |

## 10. Evidentiary ceiling

**What the pages read can support:**

- That a CIA operational cable of January 2000 stated Mihdhar's travel documents, including a multiple entry US visa, "had been copied and passed 'to the FBI for further investigation'" — as quoted by the DOJ OIG from the cable (G5).
- That the DOJ OIG and the CIA OIG, both with access to the records and the participants, **found no document and no witness corroborating that the documents were in fact passed** (G14, G17).
- That the notification vehicle addressed to the FBI was put on hold in writing about three hours earlier (G4, G8), was never sent (G13), and that the information did not reach the FBI until shortly before the attacks (G13).
- That the one contemporaneous e-mail written expressly to record what the FBI had been told **omits the passport and visa** (G18), and the FBI recipient's contemporaneous note omits it too (G19).

**What the pages read cannot support:**

- **That anyone intended the information not to reach the FBI.** The OIG makes no such finding, and an uncorroborated assertion in a cable is consistent with a good-faith relay of something the author was told — which the author herself offered as the explanation (G15) and which nothing read excludes.
- That the hold caused the fifth cable's wording, or vice versa. The time relationship is recorded; no causal claim is made by the OIG or here.
- Anything about what the Deputy Chief who directed the hold knew or wanted. He is recorded as not recalling it (G22).
- Any movement of H1 or H5. Under `04` §2.3 those require their own evidence, which these pages do not contain in either direction.
- Any characterisation of the report as a whole, 440 pages of which are unread.
