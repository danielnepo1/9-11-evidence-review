## Checkpoint 1 — `PDB-20010806` (2026-09-19)

**Read:** 2 of 2 pages, in full. OCR'd, **both pages image-verified**.

**What it settles.**
- The literal text of the 6 August 2001 PDB is now in the audit's hands for the first time, after three sessions in which it was recorded as unreadable.
- The non-corroboration caveat exists, is scoped to "some of the more sensational threat reporting" rather than to the document's reporting generally, and **is set in bold italic in the original** — the characterisation in DOC-1 is literally accurate.
- The document contains current-state items (~70 FBI full field investigations; an unresolved May 2001 UAE embassy call; "recent surveillance of federal buildings in New York") alongside its historical material.

**What it does not settle.**
- Whether the UAE call was a hoax. The document says CIA and FBI "are investigating" it and assesses nothing.
- Whether Rice's "no current information" characterisation is accurate, because the two readings of "current" are different propositions (card §8, X-b).
- Whether the bold on the caveat carries emphasis or is the article's standard layout convention for a paragraph's lead sentence. The image does not decide it (card §6.1).
- The reliability of the reporting behind three propositions, whose foreign liaison services are redacted.

**Registered:** B2-ref yes; **B2-rely yes**, subtype *custodial/cooperating-witness statement to the FBI* (Ahmed Ressam), one instance, hand-verified with exact passage.

**Nothing concluded about A10 or A16.**

## Checkpoint 2 — `MFR-TENET-20031223` (2026-09-19)

**Read:** 3 of 3 pages, in full. Text layer present but **agency-produced OCR**; all three pages image-verified (see [`decisions.md`](decisions.md) D-003).

**What it settles.**
- This document is **not a substantive 9/11 interview.** It is a luncheon meeting about access, document production and process, recorded by Chris Kojm and reviewed by Philip Zelikow, **not under oath**. Anyone expecting the DCI's account of the pre-attack period will not find it here, and the audit should stop expecting it from this document.
- On 23 December 2003 the DCI refused the Commission direct access to **seven individuals "so central to the plot"**. Reasons were given, alternatives short of physical access were offered, and the position was recorded as firm. First-hand and contemporaneous to the meeting.
- The Commission asked the CIA to certify **completion** of document production; the CIA offered to certify a **good-faith effort**. The document records the request and the different offer, and no resolution.
- The document contains **no** reference to al-Mihdhar, al-Hazmi, Kuala Lumpur, watchlisting, CIA–FBI notification, the May 1998 capture operation, the 25 March 1999 PDB, or the 6 August 2001 PDB. It therefore bears on **none** of claims A1–A5.

**What it does not settle.**
- Whether access was ultimately granted or refused. The meeting's own participants left it open ("At least for today").
- Whether document production was ever completed or certified.
- Anything about Cofer Black's "5 to 15 terrorist attacks in the United States" prediction — **its own authors disagreed in the document about which President heard it and in which year** (card §6.1). Bush in summer 2001 and Clinton in late 1999 are materially different propositions.
- Whether T20's "no data, no information coming back about domestic threats" conflicts with the PDB's FBI items, or describes a different subject entirely (CIA collection vs FBI investigative activity). Unresolved pending the CIA–FBI channel question.

**Registered:** B2-ref yes; **B2-rely no** — the document discusses detainee reporting and rests no factual claim on it. This is what forced the two-column split in [`decisions.md`](decisions.md) D-002.

**Nothing concluded about any claim or target.**

### Value assessment, recorded before the next reading

The highest-value item in this document is procedural, not substantive: a dated, first-hand record of an access refusal and of a certification substitution, both bearing on ST-4 / T-4.4. The second is methodological: the bracketed staff divergence at T18 is a documented instance of two trained note-takers hearing one statement differently, which is a caution that applies to **every** MFR in this corpus, including the three already treated as read in earlier phases.
