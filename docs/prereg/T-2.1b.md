## 1. Purpose

X-001 rests on one secondary source. Its genealogy field reads *"independent as to records, single-channel as to transmission"* — this audit reads every record through the DOJ OIG's quotations. Two other bodies reconstructed the same records from the same underlying material, earlier and independently: the Joint Inquiry (report dated December 2002) and the 9/11 Commission (July 2004). Reading them either widens the channel or exposes divergence. Either outcome is informative; neither is a foregone conclusion.

## 2. The four questions — fixed before reading

| # | Question | What settles it |
|---|---|---|
| **Q-a** | Does each body mention the fifth cable's assertion that Mihdhar's documents "had been passed to the FBI"? | Presence or absence of that specific assertion in the relevant pages. **Absence is recorded as absence**, never as denial (`cards/README.md` rule 4) |
| **Q-b** | What calendar date does each body assign to (i) the drafting of the CIR, (ii) the hold note, (iii) the fifth cable? | The DOJ OIG pages read say only "the same morning/day" relative to 5 January 2000. A body that gives a date pins something the OIG passage did not |
| **Q-c** | Does each body mention the hold note and attribute it to the Deputy Chief of the Bin Laden Unit? | Presence, wording and attribution |
| **Q-d** | Where does the CIA's explanation — that the information reached the FBI "through another cable" — first appear? | The DOJ OIG records it as a CIA assertion at fn. 274. If the JI (2002) or CR (2004) carries the same explanation, it predates the OIG's rejection of it. If neither does, the explanation is first attested in the OIG's November 2004 report |

## 3. Outcomes — fixed before reading

| Outcome | Criterion |
|---|---|
| **Divergence** | Two bodies assign incompatible dates, attributions, or presence/absence on any of Q-a to Q-d, on the same proposition at the same scope — tested through the seven-part gate in `contradiction-ledger.md` §1 before being called a divergence. → New entry **X-002**, classed `direct contradiction`, `material tension`, `scope mismatch` or `revision` as the gate determines |
| **Convergence with independent basis** | Bodies agree **and** each cites its own basis (its own access to the cable system, its own interviews). → X-001 genealogy upgraded from single-channel to multi-channel; A-001 M3 updated |
| **Convergence with shared ancestor** | Bodies agree but one visibly relies on the other (citation, identical wording, identical pseudonym scheme). → Agreement recorded but **not** counted as corroboration (`05` §3.2) |
| **Silence** | A body does not treat the episode at the level of detail needed for Q-a to Q-d. → Recorded as absence; no inference |

## 4. Ceilings — not adjustable after

- A-001 and X-001 stay at escalation **≤2**. Three descriptions of one episode are not three anomalies; level 3 requires substantially independent anomalies, and this is one.
- Divergence between bodies on a date or an attribution is evidence about the *reconstructions*, not about the events. It is not evidence of intent by anyone in January 2000.
- If a body omits the fifth cable, the omission is recorded. It does not establish that the body suppressed it; an inquiry can reasonably leave out a record it judged immaterial, and the OIG's own treatment (fn. 274) shows the cable became salient only as the CIA's explanation.
