# Pre-registration — T-2.1: the January 2000 notification chain

> **Written before any source was read.** Committed before the first document of this target was opened, so that what counts as a positive, negative or inconclusive result cannot be adjusted after seeing the evidence. Any later change to this file must be a dated amendment, never a silent edit.

Target: **T-2.1**, [`../05-stress-test-execution-plan.md`](../05-stress-test-execution-plan.md) §4 ST-2. Priority **P0**.
Pre-registered: **20 September 2026**, before reading.

---

## 1. The proposition under test

> In or around January 2000, CIA personnel prepared a notification to the FBI concerning Khalid al-Mihdhar and/or Nawaf al-Hazmi, and an internal record subsequently indicated that the notification had been transmitted when it had not been.

This is deliberately narrow. It is a claim about **the existence and content of records**, not about anyone's intent, and not about what anyone knew. The wider questions — why the information was not passed, why neither man was watchlisted before August 2001 — are ST-2's, not this target's.

---

## 2. Why this target and not another

Every other item in the intelligence track rests on 2003–04 recollection. If a record of this kind exists, it is **contemporaneous**: created in January 2000, by the people doing the work, before anyone knew it would matter. A contemporaneous document that says a thing happened which did not happen does not depend on anyone's memory, candour or later characterisation. That is what makes this the highest-value single artifact in the track.

---

## 3. What counts as what — fixed before reading

| Outcome | Criteria |
|---|---|
| **Positive** | A contemporaneous record (cable, message, draft, routing slip, database entry) dated on or about January 2000, whose text or metadata states or indicates that a notification to the FBI was sent, **together with** evidence in the same or another contemporaneous record that no such notification was in fact transmitted. Both halves required, both from primary or IG-reconstructed records, not from a witness's later recollection |
| **Negative** | Contemporaneous records show the notification was drafted and then **openly** not sent (held, cancelled, superseded, or routed elsewhere), with no record asserting transmission; **or** records show it was drafted and actually transmitted |
| **Inconclusive** | The pattern is attested only by later recollection or by an investigative body's summary that this audit cannot trace to an underlying record; **or** the underlying records are withheld, destroyed or unlocated |

**Note on sources.** The DOJ Inspector General's reconstruction is a **secondary** source for this purpose: it is an investigative body describing records. It is strong secondary evidence, but if the audit relies on it, the finding is at the strength of "the DOJ IG found X", not "the record says X". Where the IG quotes a record verbatim, that quotation is treated as the strongest available proxy for the record and is recorded as such — explicitly, with the distinction preserved in the card.

## 4. Conclusion ceiling — fixed before reading, not adjustable after

Carried verbatim from [`../05`](../05-stress-test-execution-plan.md) §4:

> Such a record supports **"false internal record; cause undetermined"**. It does **not** by itself support intent, and a routine drafting or marking failure predicts the same artifact.

Escalation on the [`../anomaly-register.md`](../anomaly-register.md) §2.1 ladder: **level 1 on entry.** Level 2 requires that ordinary reconciliation be tested and found inadequate. Level 4 (evidence of concealment) requires affirmative evidence of deliberate withholding, which a discrepancy between a record and reality does not supply on its own.

**Ordinary explanations that must be tested and reported before any escalation**, stated now so the list cannot be trimmed later:

1. A draft marked or logged as sent by the drafting system when the send step was never completed — a workflow artifact, not a statement by any person.
2. A person recording an intention as an accomplishment, in good faith, under load.
3. Notification made through a different channel, so that a record of "sent" is true of a different transmission than the one being looked for.
4. Records of two different documents conflated by later reconstruction.
5. A supervisory or release step that failed silently.

If any of these fits the evidence as well as a deliberate act does, the entry stays at level 1 and says so.

---

## 5. Reading order

1. **DOJ IG**, *A Review of the FBI's Handling of Intelligence Information Related to the September 11 Attacks* — the most detailed public reconstruction of the notification chain. Primary target for verbatim quotations of underlying records.
2. **`JI-2002`** (Joint Inquiry, in corpus) — an independent institutional reconstruction, produced earlier and by a different body. Divergence between the two is itself informative.
3. **`CR-2004`** (9/11 Commission Report, in corpus) — the third reconstruction, and the one whose endnotes may point at the underlying records by name.
4. Underlying records themselves, if any are locatable in released form.

Sources 1–3 are three separate bodies describing the same events. Under [`../05`](../05-stress-test-execution-plan.md) §3.2, agreement between them is **not** automatically corroboration: they may share an ancestor. Where they agree, the genealogy is checked before the agreement is counted.

---

## 6. What a positive result would and would not license

Written now, because this is the target most likely to be over-read.

**Would license:** an entry in the contradiction ledger classed `direct contradiction` between a record and the event it records, with contemporaneous provenance; and a demand, on the record, for the cause.

**Would not license:** any statement that the failure was deliberate; any statement about what anyone knew; any inference that the same pattern occurred elsewhere; any movement of H1 or H5. Those require their own evidence, and this target does not supply it in either direction.
