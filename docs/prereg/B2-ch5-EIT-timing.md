> **Written after location-only reconnaissance (page numbers and keyword co-occurrence, no dates or quotations fixed) and before any date or quotation was extracted from `SSCI-2014` for classification.** Reconnaissance established that the document is the Executive Summary/Findings and Conclusions (712 pages, ABBYY-OCR'd, D-003 applies), that it states specific capture and EIT-cessation dates for at least KSM and Abu Zubaydah in narrative prose (no per-detainee table), and that Nashiri, Hambali, Ramzi bin al-Shibh (SSCI's spelling for Binalshibh) and Khallad/Tawfiq bin Attash have not yet had any date fixed.

## 1. The question

For each of the 54 chapter-5 notes classified `CIA-DETENTION` in the closed census (`11`), cross-reference the note's own cited interrogation date(s) against that detainee's SSCI-documented enhanced-interrogation-technique (EIT) timeline, and classify:

| Status | Criterion |
|---|---|
| **DURING-EIT** | The cited interrogation date falls within the detainee's SSCI-documented EIT application window (capture/first EIT use → SSCI-stated cessation date) |
| **POST-EIT** | The cited interrogation date is after the SSCI-stated cessation date for that detainee |
| **NEVER-SUBJECTED** | SSCI does not document that detainee as ever subjected to EITs (per SSCI's own accounting) — the DURING/POST axis does not apply |
| **INCONCLUSIVE** | SSCI does not state a clear cessation date for that detainee, or the detainee's identity/timeline cannot be pinned from what is read |

This is a **provenance-timing fact**, not a reliability verdict. The conclusion ceiling in §5 is fixed now and governs every classification below.

## 2. Facts to be established before classification — sourced, quoted, image-verified

For each of the six detainees, from `SSCI-2014` only (not from prior general knowledge):

2. **Whether SSCI documents EITs being used against that detainee at all** — SSCI's own count is 39 of 119 detainees; most were never subjected to EITs.

Each is read cold in the next step with no date assumed in advance.

## 4. Verification standard

`SSCI-2014`'s PDF producer is `ABBYY FineReader 10` — **agency-side OCR**, D-003 applies in full. Every date and quotation used for classification is checked against the rendered page image before being recorded. The extracted text in this document is visibly degraded in places (e.g., "T have forgotten" for "I have forgotten", "March I, 2003" for "March 1, 2003" already seen in reconnaissance) — decisive digits are never trusted from OCR alone.

## 5. Conclusion ceiling — fixed before classification, verbatim, carried from `11` §10 and extended

> A `DURING-EIT` classification shows that the cited interrogation report's date falls within the window SSCI documents that detainee as having been subjected to coercive technique. **It does not show that the specific statement cited in the Commission Report's note is false, unreliable, or was itself obtained coercively** — SSCI's own reporting is about technique application periods, not a statement-by-statement reliability audit, and a `DURING-EIT` statement may still be accurate. A `POST-EIT` classification shows the opposite timing fact and carries the same non-inference: it does not certify the statement's truth either. **`NEVER-SUBJECTED` is not evidence the CIA's programme was benign** — it is a fact about that one individual, reported by SSCI, which documents extensive abuse of others. No classification here may be cited as resolving whether any specific claim used in `CR-2004` chapter 5 is true.

## 6. Expectation, stated in advance

Given that KSM was subjected to extensive, well-documented EITs (SSCI's most extensively covered subject) starting immediately upon capture (1 March 2003) and that the majority of KSM-attributed citations in the 54-note set carry 2003 dates spanning the year, **a material share of the KSM citations are expected to classify `DURING-EIT` or fall shortly after cessation**, while later citations (into 2004) are expected to classify `POST-EIT`. **If instead the reporting is concentrated in a narrow, clearly-post-cessation window, that is reported with equal prominence** — it would materially weaken the timing concern for this detainee specifically.

## 7. What this cross-reference does not do

It does not read the full SSCI report (712 pages); it reads only the passages needed to fix each detainee's capture/cessation facts, located by targeted search and confirmed by image. It does not resolve historical or legal disputes about whether any technique constitutes torture — that is SSCI's own finding, not re-litigated here. It does not extend to the 30 `rely=no` notes from `11`, which by definition do not rest on detainee reporting.
